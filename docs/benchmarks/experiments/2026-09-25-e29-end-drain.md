# E29: no speculative step past a possible length finish

**Recorded outcome: promote.** The owner accepted it; it is the
[September 25 E29 reference](../baselines/2026-09-25-e29.md). It was compared with the frozen
[September 25 E28b medians](../baselines/2026-09-25-e28b.md).

E28b's seven draft tokens improved code decode but slowed the first token of a request that
arrives just after another one ends. With asynchronous scheduling the engine queues a
request's next step before the output of the step in flight has returned. The vendor guard
skips that step only when a single token is missing, so a request limited by `max_tokens`
usually finishes inside a step that could produce several tokens. The step queued behind it
then verifies drafts for a finished request, and the next request waits for it. The standard
suite submits its one-request rounds and each cached replay exactly this way.

The [overlay](../../../scripts/node/experiments/e03/end-drain/README.md) changes only request
boundaries:

- **Hold near the length limit (`VLLM_E29_END_DRAIN=1`).** The scheduler does not queue
  another step while the step in flight may finish the request.
- **Idle coalescing (`VLLM_E29_IDLE_COALESCE_MS=4`).** At an idle engine, requests arriving
  together are prefilled in one step. The hold leaves the engine idle when the next group of
  requests arrives, so without this window C2 and C4 rounds started staggered in 22 of 24
  cohorts.

## Diagnosis

The probes used the frozen Rigmark client on an idle service.

- **E28b.** Over 40 length-capped predecessors, the next request reached its first token in
  0.335 s at the median when the predecessor's last step emitted one token. Otherwise it took
  0.404 s, with a maximum of 0.439 s.
- **Hold only (0 ms window).** The next request reached its first token in 0.336 s whatever
  the last step. Rounds submitted back to back took 0.336 s against 0.392 s on E28b.
- **Arrival spread.** Measured on the engine, arrivals in 21 two- and four-request cohorts
  spread over 1.45 ms at the median and 3.17 ms at most. The 4 ms window covers all of them.
  With it, 0 of 24 cohorts started staggered.

## Measurement

Three complete native suites ran on one load: hold on, 4 ms window, trace off. Launcher
arguments matched the overlay on all four ranks, and both post-boot gates passed 3 s after
`/health` 200. Integrity: 162/162 requests, zero errors, output gates 45/45. E28b was
restored afterwards.

## Prefill re-runs

The owner asked to re-run the prefill section where the suites showed anomalous slowdowns:
three 64K replays at 1.77–1.97 s and a slower 8K cold median in suite 3. The re-run used
three native executions of the frozen Rigmark with the suite settings, `--runs 1` (the
minimum decode section) and `--skip-concurrency`, on a second load B. There were zero errors
and 54/54 exact prompt lengths.

The three suites remain the formal measurement. The supplementary medians of all six
executions are below, as time to first token:

| Prefill | E29 (6 executions) | E28b | E27c |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| 8K cold / replay | 3.200 s / 0.925 s | 3.202 s / 0.945 s | 3.201 s / 0.869 s |
| 32K cold / replay | 11.866 s / 0.966 s | 11.962 s / 0.985 s | 12.185 s / 0.884 s |
| 64K cold / replay | 24.491 s / 1.650 s | 24.338 s / 1.668 s | 24.759 s / 1.641 s |

The 64K replay spikes did not recur: all nine re-run replays took 1.635–1.673 s.

## What remains

Cached replay recovers only part of E28b's loss. Each replay waited 6–170 ms for the
SparkCache connector to confirm on all four ranks that the cold request's context had been
published; all 27 replays in the measured load did.

Seven draft tokens end the eight-token cold request sooner, so the replay arrives earlier in
that publication. At 64K, six of nine replays took 1.62–1.64 s, the fastest of the three
recipes, and three took 1.77–1.97 s.

Other measurements:

- **EOS-finished requests.** A request that ends on EOS can still leave one step behind it.
  As on E27c and E28b, the next request then waits about 0.40 s.
- **Interference (protocol 2).** Against the E27c reference, decode in the interference
  window was equal or higher in every cell. The arriving 8K prompt with three agents took
  4.39 s against 3.74 s, in one execution.
- **Four simultaneous 32K prompts.** Per-stream decode was 15.7 tok/s against 13.7 on E27c.
- **Memory.** Rank 0 kept at least 1.95 GiB available.
