# E28b: E28 with a 16 GiB KV pool

**Recorded outcome: promote.** The owner accepted it; it is the
[September 25 E28b reference](../baselines/2026-09-25-e28b.md).

E28 left 4.88 full 262,144-token contexts in the 15 GiB pool, so five agents at the full
context no longer fit together. The owner asked to raise the pool to 16 GiB, noting that the
earlier 16 GiB failure on September 19 ran six parallel agents rather than five. The
[overlay](../../../scripts/node/experiments/e03/kv-16gib/README.md) changes only
`--kv-cache-memory-bytes` to 17,179,869,184.

## Measurement

One coordinated transition from E28, both post-boot gates 3.5 s after `/health` 200, then
three complete native suites on one retained load with MemAvailable sampled once per second
on every rank. Integrity: 162/162 requests with zero errors, gates 45/45, no lost client
probe.

- **Capacity:** 1,365,066 KV tokens, 5.21 full contexts.
- **Memory:** minimum available during the suites 2.12 GiB on rank 0 and at least 6.05 GiB
  on ranks 1–3; `earlyoom` acts below 0.5 GiB.
- **Performance:** every metric within 3% of E28, so the larger pool does not change
  performance. The [baseline report](../baselines/2026-09-25-e28b.md) lists all 16 metrics
  against E27c.
